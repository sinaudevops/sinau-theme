Hugo is one of the fastest static site generators available. Combined with GitHub Pages for free hosting, it is the ideal setup for a personal technical blog.

## Why Hugo?

Hugo is written in Go, which makes its build process incredibly fast. A blog with hundreds of articles can be built in seconds. Some of Hugo's advantages:

1. **Extreme Speed**: Build times are measured in milliseconds.
2. **Robust Templating**: Flexible and powerful Go templates.
3. **Built-in Features**: Includes taxonomies, menus, and more out of the box.

In this tutorial, we will walk through the process from installation to deployment.
